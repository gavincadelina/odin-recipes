Pancit Canton

Description

Pancit canton is a classic Filipino stir-fried egg noodle dish tossed with sliced meat, crisp vegetables, and a savory soy-citrus sauce. It is traditionally served at birthdays and family celebrations to symbolize long life and good health.

Serve warm with fresh calamansi or lemon wedges squeezed over the top for brightness.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried pancit canton (flour stick noodles)
  • 1/2 lb chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup cabbage, shredded
  • 1 medium carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup snap peas, trimmed
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 4 pieces calamansi or 1 lemon, halved

Steps

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a large wok or skillet over medium-high heat and sauté the garlic and onions until fragrant.
  2. Add the sliced chicken and cook until lightly browned on all sides.
  3. Toss in the carrots, snap peas, and cabbage, stir-frying for 2 minutes until slightly tender, then remove the vegetables and meat onto a plate.
  4. Pour the chicken broth, soy sauce, and oyster sauce into the same wok and bring to a simmer.
  5. Add the dried pancit canton noodles and cook, tossing gently, until the noodles absorb all the liquid and soften.
  6. Return the cooked chicken and vegetables to the wok, toss everything together for 1 minute, and serve with calamansi on the side.
