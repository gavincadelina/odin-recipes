Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken adobo is a classic Filipino dish made by braising chicken in vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and black peppercorns. The simmering process tenderizes the meat while creating a rich, savory glaze.

Serve this dish hot over steamed white rice to soak up the flavorful sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken thighs, bone-in
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup white vinegar
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 3 dried bay leaves
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Steps

  1. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a bowl and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and brown the chicken pieces on both sides.
  3. Pour the remaining marinade, vinegar, peppercorns, and bay leaves into the skillet.
  4. Bring the liquid to a boil without stirring, then lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 30 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  5. Remove the lid and simmer for an additional 5 minutes to thicken the sauce before serving.
