Pork Sinigang

Description

Pork sinigang is a comforting Filipino sour soup made with tender pork belly, fresh vegetables, and a tangy tamarind broth. It is a staple dish known for its refreshing, savory-sour flavor profile.

Enjoy it piping hot alongside steamed white rice and a small saucer of fish sauce for dipping.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs pork belly, cut into cubes
  • 1 packet tamarind soup base (sinigang mix)
  • 2 medium tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 1 cup daikon radish (labanos), sliced
  • 1 cup string beans (sitaw), cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 bunch water spinach (kangkong)
  • 2 pieces long green chili peppers (siling haba)
  • 8 cups water
  • Fish sauce (patis) to taste

Steps

  1. Pour water into a large pot and bring it to a boil over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the pork belly, tomatoes, and onions, then lower the heat and simmer for 45 minutes until the pork is tender.
  3. Stir in the tamarind soup base and radish, cooking for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the string beans and green chili peppers, simmering for another 3 minutes.
  5. Season with fish sauce to taste, turn off the heat, and stir in the water spinach so it wilts in the residual heat before serving.
